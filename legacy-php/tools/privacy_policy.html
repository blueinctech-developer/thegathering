Who we are

This website is run by The Gathering International Christian Network, incorporated in the United Kingdom (Registration No. 15843980), an arm of The Chariot of Fire World Outreach (COFWO). You can contact us at thegatheringcof@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us through the forms on this website:

  • Registration: your name, email address, phone number, city, church or ministry, the number of people attending and any notes you add.
  • Contact and partnership enquiries: your name, email address, phone number (optional), organisation (optional) and your message.

To protect the website from abuse, we also store a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address and your browser type with each submission.

How we use it

  • To manage your registration for The Gathering and contact you about it.
  • To reply to your message or partnership enquiry.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who can see it

Only authorised members of our ministry team, through a password-protected admin area. Emails are sent through our email provider, and the website is hosted by Hostinger.

How long we keep it

We keep registration details for up to 12 months after the event, and enquiries for up to 24 months, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. After that they are deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it, by emailing thegatheringcof@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This website uses one essential cookie to keep forms secure. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
