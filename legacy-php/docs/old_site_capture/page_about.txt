#2 About The Gathering 
It is an annual call to prayer, revival, and transformation — bringing together ministers, families, and communities in pursuit of spiritual awakening and tangible compassion. 
Partner With Us 
#2 Who We Are 
The Gathering International Christian Network is an annual interdenominational prayer conference committed to spiritual revival and community transformation. 
Incorporated in the United Kingdom, Registration No: 15843980 
An arm of The Chariot of Fire World Outreach (COFWO) 
Convener: Yomi Adeboye 
Our mission is simple: To inspire revival, impart truth, and empower communities — spiritually and practically. 
We believe prayer must be accompanied by compassion. Worship must be followed by action. Faith must produce impact. 
#2 OUR VISION 
“Breaking Territories” is not only our 2026 theme, it is our mission. We envision: Expanding spiritual revival across communities, Providing structured medical outreach to underserved families, Feeding vulnerable households through Heart of Compassion, Developing a permanent Gathering Land for long-term ministry, Building infrastructure that supports future impact 
The Gathering Land in Ido LGA (2.66 acres) represents a long term investment in sustainable ministry and outreach. 
We are building not just for one year, but for generations. 
#2 Why The Gathering Matters 
Across many communities in Nigeria, families face ongoing challenges: Limited access to healthcare, Financial hardship and food insecurity, Spiritual and emotional burdens, Inadequate community infrastructure. 
For many, opportunities for medical
screening, basic medications, and
community support are rare.
The Gathering responds to both spiritual and
practical needs, creating an atmosphere of
prayer while delivering tangible outreach.
