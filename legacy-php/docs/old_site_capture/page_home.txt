#2 A Movement Beyond an Event 
#2 THE GATHERING 2026 
The Gathering is more than a conference. It is an annual call to prayer, revival, and transformation — bringing together ministers, families, and communities in pursuit of spiritual awakening and tangible compassion. 
Each year, lives are touched through worship, the Word, healing prayer, community feeding, and medical outreach 
In 2026, under the theme “Breaking Territories,” we believe God is calling us to expand our impact — spiritually and practically —establishing a permanent foundation for future generations. 
The Gathering International Christian Network is an annual 
interdenominational prayer conference committed to spiritual revival 
and community transformation. 
Incorporated in the United Kingdom Registration No: 15843980 
An arm of The Chariot of Fire World Outreach (COFWO) 
Convener: Yomi Adeboye 
Our mission is simple: 
To inspire revival, impart truth, and empower communities — spiritually and practically. 
We believe prayer must be accompanied by compassion. Worship must be followed by action. Faith must produce impact 
#2 Why The Gathering Matters 
Across many communities in Nigeria, families face ongoing challenges: Limited access to healthcare, Financial hardship and food insecurity, Spiritual and emotional burdens, Inadequate community infrastructure. 
For many, opportunities for medical
screening, basic medications, and
community support are rare.
The Gathering responds to both spiritual and
practical needs, creating an atmosphere of
prayer while delivering tangible outreach. 
#2 How The Gathering Creates Impact 
The Gathering brings together prayer and compassion to create meaningful spiritual and community transformation through outreach, service, and ministry. 
#2 Prayer & Worship 
Uniting believers in worship, prayer, healing, and spiritual renewal, and experience deeper growth. 
#2 Medical Outreach 
Providing free screenings, and essential medications to underserved communities. 
#2 Compassion Outreach 
Delivering food support and practical assistance to families facing hardship. 
#2 Community Transformation 
Strengthening communities through sustainable outreach, unity, and long-term ministry initiatives.
