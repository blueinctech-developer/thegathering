# Website admin guide (for church staff)

You don't need any technical knowledge to keep the website up to date. Everything is done at:

**https://thegatheringcof.org/admin**

## Signing in
- Use the email and password an administrator gave you.
- Forgot your password? Click **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. A link arrives by email and works for one hour.
- Change your own password any time: click your name (top right) → **My account**.
- After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes. That's a safety feature.
- You're signed out automatically after 2 hours of not using the admin.

## The dashboard
Shows the current event, how many people have registered, places left, anything waiting for you (new submissions, testimonies to review, emails that failed), and a setup checklist.

## Getting the next Gathering ready
1. **Events → Add event** (or edit the current one): name, theme, dates, venue, map location, description, homepage photo, flyer, programme PDF.
2. Tick **This is the current event**. The homepage, countdown, registration and schedule all follow this event.
3. Tick **Registration is open** when you're ready. Untick it to close registration. Optional **Capacity** closes registration automatically when full.
4. **Speakers → Add speaker** for each minister (photo, title, bio). Use the ↑ ↓ arrows to change the order. Tick "Feature on the homepage" for the main speakers.
5. **Schedule → Add day**, then **+ Add session** under each day.

The countdown uses the event's start date. Leave dates blank until they are confirmed — never put in a guess.

## Pages
**Pages** holds About, Venue & Accommodation, Give & Partner, Volunteer and Privacy Policy. Each one stays hidden until you tick **Published**. You can also add new pages (e.g. "Our history").

The text editor buttons: **B** bold, *I* italic, H2/H3 headings, lists, quote, Link. Paste from Word or WhatsApp is cleaned automatically.

## Photos
- **Gallery → Add album** (title, year), **Save**, then **Choose photos to upload** (you can pick many at once), then **Upload**.
- Under each photo, add a **caption** and a short **description** (what's in the photo — this helps blind visitors and Google). Example: "Congregation worshipping during the Saturday evening service".
- Photos are resized and optimised automatically. Phone photos are fine.
- Only use photos the ministry owns or has permission to use.

## Media & sermons
**Media & sermons → Add message**: paste the YouTube/Facebook/audio link, add the title, speaker and date. YouTube thumbnails appear automatically.
The livestream link and the "We are live right now" switch are in **Settings → Livestream & giving**.

## News
**News → Add news post**. Leave it as **Draft** while you write; choose **Published** to put it on the site. A future publish date schedules it.

## Testimonies
Testimonies sent through the website arrive as **Pending**. Read them, fix spelling if needed (keep the meaning), then **Approve** — only approved ones appear on the site. Never publish a testimony without the person's permission (the form asks for it).

## Submissions (registrations, contact, prayer, volunteers…)
- **Submissions** lists everything sent through the website. Use the tabs to see one form type, and the search box for a name, email, phone or reference number.
- Open a submission to see all details, change its **status** (New → In progress → Handled), add **team notes**, reply by email, or **mark a registration as checked in** at the door.
- **Export to Excel (CSV)** downloads what you're currently viewing.
- **Prayer requests and personal details are confidential.** Don't forward them outside the team, and don't leave exported files on shared computers.

### If the dashboard says "email failed"
The submission is safe — only the email didn't send. Open **Email log** (administrators) or the submission, and click **Retry**. Emails also retry automatically every 10 minutes. If they keep failing, tell your administrator.

## Bank account details (Give page)
Open **Bank accounts** in the menu, then **+ Add block**:
- **Title:** a name for the account, e.g. *UK account* or *Naira account*.
- **Text:** one detail per line, written as *Label: value*, for example:
  `Bank: …` · `Account name: …` · `Account number: …` · `Sort code: …`
Each account appears on the Give page as its own card with a **Copy details** button. Use the arrows to change the order, untick **Show on the website** to hide one. **Check every digit before saving.**
The online giving link (if you have one) is in **Settings → Livestream & giving**.

## Settings (administrators only)
Logo and colours, contact details, social media links, livestream, giving and bank details, which forms are switched on, where notification emails go, search & sharing image, and analytics codes.
**Double-check bank details digit by digit before saving.**

## Staff accounts (administrators only)
**Staff accounts → Add account**. Choose:
- **Editor** — can update content and handle submissions.
- **Administrator** — can also change settings, accounts and redirects.
Give the starting password to the person privately (in person or by phone), and ask them to change it in **My account**. Disable an account the day someone leaves the team.

## Need help?
Contact your website administrator. Nothing you do in the admin can break the website's design, and every important change is recorded in the **Activity log**.
