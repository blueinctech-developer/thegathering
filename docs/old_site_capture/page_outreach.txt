#4 The Gathering 2026 
#2 FUEL THE MISSION. 
This is not just a conference. It is a strategic mobilization. Your support directly funds the front-line outreach programs and medical aid initiatives. 
Partner With Us 
#2 Strategic Aid Programs 
Direct action where it matters most. Every program is vetted for maximum ground-level impact. 
#2 Spiritual Outreach 
Disseminating the message of hope through large-scale events and personalized ministry teams. The Gathering is an annual interdenominational prayer conference committed to spiritual revival and tangible community transformation. 
#2 Welfare Outreach 
Food distribution and practical support for vulnerable families. 
#2 The Gathering Land 
In order to expand the impact of The Gathering and create a permanent foundation for future ministry and outreach, land has been secured in Ido Local Government Area, Oyo State, Nigeria. The land spans approximately 2.66 Acres (10,783 m²). 
#2 Medical Aid 
As part of The Gathering 2026, a dedicated Medical Outreach Programme will provide free health screening, consultation, and basic medication distribution to support community well-being. Our goal is to reach up to 5,000 people during the outreach programme.
